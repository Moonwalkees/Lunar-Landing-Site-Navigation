# 8.2 Sun and Earth connection sounds

Status: proposed
Owner:
Source: X-factors (team brainstorm, not yet in scope.md)

## Description
Play sounds as Sun and Earth come into or out of view.
- Earth: telecomm beeps and boops fading in or out.
- Sun: a "solar" shine sound fading in or out.

## Expected user interaction
As the scrubber plays, the user hears beeps fade in when Earth comes into view and a shine sound when the Sun does, fading out when each is lost.

## Done when
- Earth and Sun sounds fade in/out on visibility changes
- Sound can be muted
