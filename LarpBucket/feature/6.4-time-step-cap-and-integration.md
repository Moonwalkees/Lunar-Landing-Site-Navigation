# 6.4 Time step cap and per-step power integration

Status: open
Owner:
Source: scope.md > Core 2 Date range (adjustable time step), Model (Battery)
Priority: high
Depends on: 3.2, 3.6

## Description
Power is sampled at step instants but multiplied by `dt`, so a short sunlit window between two samples can be missed or counted for a whole step. Cap `step_hours` in the model/API (e.g. max 0.25 h, min set by BUG-002's fix) or sub-sample power within each step, then integrate. Reject or clamp values beyond the cap with a clear message. Document the default.

## Why it is useful
The user can adjust the time step (scope Core 2). Coarse steps are tempting for long windows, but results then depend on the step chosen.

## Impact of not having it
Longest night, % sunlit and battery minimum change with the step size. At the poles, where grazing sunlight appears and disappears within hours, a coarse step can hide a brief blackout or brief charging window and flip "survives".

## Expected user interaction
User picks any step in the allowed range and gets nearly identical metrics. Values outside the range are rejected or clamped with a message.

## Done when
- Same site and window at 0.25 h and 0.5 h steps differ by less than an agreed tolerance on key metrics
- Out-of-range `step_hours` handled (ties in with BUG-002)
- Cap documented in scope.md
