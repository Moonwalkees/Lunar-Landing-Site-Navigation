# 6.5 Sun disc azimuth-width sampling

Status: open
Owner:
Source: scope.md > Model (Sun disc fraction)
Priority: medium
Depends on: 2.2, 3.1

## Description
`disc_fraction` takes the skyline at the Sun's centre azimuth only. The disc spans ~0.53° of azimuth, and the skyline is 0.5° resolution. Use the maximum skyline elevation within `sun_az ± 0.267°` (or a mean over a few samples) as `skyline_el` before applying the 3.1 formula. Keep the 3.1 function signature and add the sampling as a helper in `model.py`.

## Why it is useful
At the poles the Sun skims the horizon, so the disc often straddles a ridge peak and a lower saddle at the same time. A single sample at the centre azimuth misjudges the visible area.

## Impact of not having it
Sunrise/sunset times at ridge edges are off by minutes to tens of minutes, and "% time sunlit" is slightly biased. Errors are small per event but accumulate across a lunar month.

## Expected user interaction
No visible change in the UI except slightly smoother sunrise/sunset transitions behind peaks.

## Done when
- Helper returns the max skyline elevation across the Sun's azimuth span, with wrap-around at 0/360° handled
- A synthetic skyline with a narrow spike next to the Sun centre reduces `f` versus centre-only sampling (unit test)
