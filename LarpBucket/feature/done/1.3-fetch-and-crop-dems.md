# 1.3 Fetch and crop LOLA DEMs

Status: done
Owner: Tony Pham
Source: tech-stack.md > Stack (Terrain), Build order 1
Note: `fetch_data.py dems` downloads both LOLA DEMs + labels and writes `ldem_85s_20m_crop.tif` (23 MB) and `ldem_64.tif` (311 MB) to `backend/data/dem/`; checks in `backend/tests/test_dems.py`.

## Description
Extend `fetch_data.py` with a `dems` command: download `LDEM_85S_20M` (polar, 20 m) and `LDEM_64` (global, ~473 m) from the PDS Geosciences node, with their labels. Crop the polar DEM to the area around the preset south pole sites to keep the size down. Save as GeoTIFF in `backend/data/dem/`.

## Expected user interaction
Developer runs `python scripts/fetch_data.py dems`; cropped GeoTIFFs appear and open with `rasterio`.

## Done when
- Both DEMs open in `rasterio` with correct CRS (polar stereographic / equirectangular)
- Height at a known point (e.g. Shackleton rim) is plausible
- Total size is small enough to bake into a Docker image (aim < 400 MB)

## Solution
**Chosen:** A `dems` subcommand in `backend/scripts/fetch_data.py`. It downloads `LDEM_85S_20M` and `LDEM_64` (`.LBL` + `.IMG`) from the PDS Geosciences LOLA GDR directory into `backend/data/raw/`, reusing 1.1's `.part`-then-rename `download()`. `rasterio`/GDAL then opens each detached PDS label. The polar DEM is cropped to the bounding box of the three south pole presets (Shackleton, Connecting Ridge, Malapert, listed in `POLAR_CROP_SITES`) plus `POLAR_CROP_MARGIN_M` = 30 km, giving 3950 x 9672 px (79 x 193 km). `LDEM_64` is kept whole. Both are written as tiled (512 px), DEFLATE + predictor-2 int16 GeoTIFFs that keep the raw DN with `scale = 0.5` and `units = m` (height above the 1737.4 km sphere). Outputs: `ldem_85s_20m_crop.tif` 23 MB, `ldem_64.tif` 311 MB, 334 MB total. Re-running skips existing outputs.

Verified (`backend/tests/test_dems.py`, 14 checks): CRS is `+proj=stere +lat_0=-90 +R=1737400` (pole at the label's centre pixel) and `+proj=eqc +lon_0=180 +R=1737400`. The crop is bit-identical to the raw IMG. Heights: pole/Shackleton rim ~ +1290 m, Shackleton floor -2789 m, Connecting Ridge 1956 m, Malapert 5111 m, Apollo 11 -1928 m. Polar and global DEMs agree within 13 m at the presets.

**Strengths**
- Lossless: int16 + scale keeps the exact source values at half the size of float32.
- Small enough for the Docker image (< 400 MB) while keeping 20 m detail near every polar preset and the whole Moon at 474 m for custom sites and the far horizon, as tech-stack.md describes.
- Tiled GeoTIFFs allow cheap windowed reads for the horizon ray-march (2.1/2.2).
- Same safe-to-re-run behaviour as the kernels (atomic writes, skip existing).

**Limitations**
- Readers must apply `ds.scales[0]`; rasterio does not apply it automatically (noted on 2.1).
- The global raster's seam is at lon 0/360, so lon exactly 0 rounds to column -1. The sampler must wrap columns (noted on 2.1).
- The polar crop only covers the current presets + 30 km. A custom polar site outside it, or a new polar preset, falls back to 474 m data until `POLAR_CROP_SITES` is updated and `data/dem/ldem_85s_20m_crop.tif` is deleted and regenerated.
- `POLAR_CROP_SITES` duplicates the presets in `frontend/src/sites.ts`. It should read `backend/app/sites.py` once 3.5 exists.
- `data/raw/` keeps ~950 MB of source files so re-cropping needs no re-download. It must be excluded from the Docker image (noted on 5.1).
- Uncovered while testing: the Shackleton preset is the crater floor, not the rim (BUG-003).

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| float32 metres | Doubles the size; global alone would be ~600 MB compressed | Readers skip the scale step |
| Keep the whole 20 m polar DEM (to -85°) | 460 MB raw on its own, over budget with the global DEM | Covers any polar custom site at 20 m |
| Crop the global DEM to the south | Custom sites anywhere need it (scope: "any lat/lon") | Smaller image |
| `LDEM_16` (1.9 km) for global | Coarser than the stack specifies; far-horizon ridges get smeared | ~16x smaller |
| Delete `data/raw/` after conversion | Changing the crop would need a ~1 GB re-download | Saves ~950 MB of local disk |
