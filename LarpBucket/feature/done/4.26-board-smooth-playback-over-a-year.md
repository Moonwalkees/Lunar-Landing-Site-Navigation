# 4.26 Smooth playback on the board, over a year

Status: done
Owner: Aiken
Source: scope.md > Core 5 Time series (time scrubber); doc/style.md > Time dock, Rendering rules ("the time scrub ease smoothly"). Requested by a team member after using the board.
Depends on: 4.12
Note: done. `TimeDock` in `frontend/src/board/Panels.tsx` plays every frame in single analysis steps; the board opens on a 365-day window at 2 h steps; `DotChart.tsx` and `Skyline.tsx` draw their unchanging dots once.

## Description
On the board, Play moved in jumps of 6 hours, at most four times a second (once a second at the slowest speed), over a one-month window. Make it flow as it does in the classic UI, and make the default window a year so Play runs through every season.

## Expected user interaction
Press Play on the board: the scrubber, the Sun and shadows and the readouts advance continuously. The window is 365 days from today by default and plays through in about 36 seconds at the default speed (10D/S). The window, step and speed can still be changed.

## Done when
- Play advances one analysis step at a time, every frame
- The board stays above 30 updates a second while playing
- The default window is 365 days

## Solution
**Chosen:** `TimeDock` uses `requestAnimationFrame` and moves by `speed x steps per hour` each second, wrapping at the end; with reduced motion it keeps to one jump a second in whole 6 h cells. `BoardApp.tsx` starts on `{ days: 365, stepHours: 2 }` and the dock on 10D/S. `DotChart.tsx` and `Skyline.tsx` each draw their time-independent dots to an off-screen canvas once and, per step, copy it and add the cursor column or the Sun and Earth discs.

**Strengths**
- Playing went from about 13 to 45 updates a second on the board page, and from 14 to 57 (integrated graphics) or 320 (discrete) in the 3D detail view. One step cost about 90 ms; it was drawing about 40,000 dots across the two panels each time.
- 2 h steps keep the five yearly analyses to about 4 seconds (8 seconds at 1 h).
- Follows doc/style.md, which asks for the time scrub to ease while readouts change in hard steps.

**Limitations**
- scope.md gives the default window as one lunar month. The board now opens on a year; the classic UI still opens on a month. scope.md was not changed.
- At 2 h steps the slower speeds are less fluid: 12 steps a second at 1D/S.
- The first load of the board waits about 4 seconds for the year, where the month took about 1.
- The hero map still redraws on every step, which is what holds the board page at about 45.

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Keep the hard 6 h jumps | The request was for it to flow | Matches the board's stepped look |
| A year at 1 h steps | Twice the wait and download for the five sites | Smoother at slow speeds |
| Interpolate the Sun between steps | The 3D view and panels take whole steps today | Smooth at any speed and step |
