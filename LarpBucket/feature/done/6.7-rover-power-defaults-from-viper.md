# 6.7 Rover power defaults from a real polar rover (VIPER)

Status: done
Owner: Aiken
Source: scope.md > Model (Power, Battery); `backend/app/constants.py` ("Temporary demo defaults. Replace after the mission power budget is defined.")
Priority: medium
Depends on: 3.1, 3.2
Note: done. `backend/app/constants.py`: battery 5,420 Wh, load 108 W, panel 1.1 m2 at 30% (449 W in full Sun). A full battery lasts 50 hours without Sun, as NASA states for VIPER.

## Description
The battery and load defaults are placeholders (100 Wh, 100 W), so the battery runs flat within an hour of losing the Sun and "Survives" is No almost everywhere. Replace the four defaults with figures for NASA's VIPER, the rover designed for the same place and problem (solar power at the lunar south pole).

| Default | Was | Now | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DEFAULT_BATTERY_CAPACITY_WH` | 100 | 5420 | NASA: "Battery capacity (start of life @ 0C): 5,420 W-hr" |
| `DEFAULT_LOAD_W` | 100 | 108 | Derived: NASA gives "50 hours survival in low power mode"; 5420 Wh / 50 h = 108 W |
| `DEFAULT_PANEL_AREA_M2` | 1.0 | 1.1 | Derived: sized so full Sun gives NASA's "peak power of 450 watts" at 30% efficiency |
| `DEFAULT_PANEL_EFFICIENCY` | 0.3 | 0.3 | Unchanged |

Sources: NASA NTRS 20240013903, "VIPER Rover Overview and Mobility and Hardware" (LSIC 2024), slide 2; NASA Science, VIPER In Depth, Key Facts.

## Why it is useful
The headline battery metrics (battery minimum, survives yes/no) then describe a real rover and can be checked against NASA's own figure: the model's rover lasts 50 hours without Sun, as VIPER is stated to.

## Impact of not having it
Battery numbers are only illustrative, and judges who know rover power budgets will see a 100 Wh battery.

## Expected user interaction
No new controls. After Analyze, the battery card, the battery row of the time series and "Survives" use the new defaults.

## Done when
- The four constants are updated, with the source of each in a comment
- A rover in full Sun gets about 450 W
- A full battery lasts about 50 hours with no Sun
- Existing tests pass

## Solution
**Chosen:** The four defaults in `backend/app/constants.py` now describe NASA's VIPER, each with its source in a comment. Nothing else changed: `utils.simulate_battery` and `analysis.py` read the same constants as before.

**Strengths**
- Checks against NASA's own figure: with these defaults a full battery and no Sun runs flat after 50 hours.
- One real rover, built for the same place and problem, rather than round numbers.

**Limitations**
- The load is derived, not published: 5,420 Wh divided by NASA's "50 hours survival in low power mode". It is a low-power load, so "Survives" means surviving the dark while doing nothing else. A rover that is driving draws more.
- The panel area is derived to give NASA's 450 W peak at 30% efficiency.
- NASA's sources differ on darkness: the engineering deck says 50 hours, the public VIPER page says the mission ends after more than four Earth days of cold and dark. The deck's figure is used.
- Still fixed defaults, not request fields. Presets for other rovers and a custom rover are not built.

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Keep 100 Wh and 100 W | The battery emptied within an hour of shadow, so "Survives" was No almost everywhere | No change to anyone's numbers |
| Presets for several rovers | Most publish no battery capacity (checked Pragyan, Yutu-2, CubeRover) | A choice for the user |
