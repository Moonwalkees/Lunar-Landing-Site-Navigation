# 4.23 Rover status card on the terrain view

Status: done
Owner: Aiken
Source: scope.md > Core 4 Metrics, Core 5 Time series (shown at the scrubber time). Requested by the 3D view's owner; a rover readout is not named in scope.md.
Depends on: 4.2, 6.7
Note: done. `frontend/src/components/RoverStatus.tsx`, drawn over the terrain view in the classic UI (`App.tsx`). The board UI does not show it.

## Description
A small card over the top-right corner of the terrain view that says what a rover parked at the site is getting at the scrubber time: share of the Sun's disc in view, power, battery level and Earth link, with one status word.

## Expected user interaction
After Analyze, the card shows in all three terrain views of the classic UI. As the scrubber moves or Play runs it changes with the site:
- green `CHARGING` or `IN SUN, FULL`, with "shadow in N"
- amber `ON BATTERY`, with "empty in N h"
- red `BATTERY EMPTY`, with "Sun returns in N"

## Done when
- The card's numbers equal the time series at the same step
- The three states appear in order as a site goes into shadow

## Solution
**Chosen:** `RoverStatus.tsx` reads the `Analysis` already in the page (`sun.fraction`, `powerW`, `batteryWh`, `comms`) at the shared time index. `TerrainView.tsx` takes it as an `overlay` and draws it over whichever view is showing. It is an ordinary web element above the canvas, not part of the Unity build.

**Strengths**
- No sunlight is worked out here, so the card cannot disagree with the time series or the metric cards.
- Works over the bird's-eye map as well as the 3D views, and needs no Unity rebuild to change.

**Limitations**
- Classic UI only. The board UI has its own lamps and metrics panel and was left alone.
- Battery capacity is not in the `/analyze` response, so the bar is drawn against the highest battery level in the window.
- The rover is parked: it describes the analysed site only.

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Draw the readout inside Unity | A second place holding the numbers, and a rebuild for every wording change | Would move with the 3D camera |
| Work out lit or shadowed from the 3D view's own shadows | They agree with the analysis about 96% of the time, so the card and the chart would sometimes differ | No dependence on Analyze |
