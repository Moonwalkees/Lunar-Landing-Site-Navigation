# 6.8 Rover presets and the user's own rover numbers

Status: done
Owner: Aiken
Source: scope.md > Model (Power, Battery); follows 6.7. Requested by a team member: "a few presets of popular rovers, and let users fill in their own numbers, only the simple ones like power and battery".
Depends on: 6.7
Note: done. `POST /analyze` takes an optional `rover` (solarW, batteryWh, loadW) and returns the numbers it used; both UIs have a rover choice (VIPER or Custom) and three number fields. Presets are in `frontend/src/rovers.ts`.

## Description
The rover's power numbers were fixed in `backend/app/constants.py`. Let the user pick a rover preset or type their own solar power, battery size and power use, and run the analysis for that rover.

## Expected user interaction
- Board: under MISSION WINDOW, choose VIPER (NASA) or CUSTOM, or type into SOLAR · W, BATTERY · WH, POWER USE · W (typing switches to CUSTOM), then RUN. Every site is re-analysed for that rover.
- Classic: a Rover dropdown and the same three fields in the site form, then Analyze.
The metric cards, battery chart and rover readout follow the rover chosen.

## Done when
- A preset fills all three numbers; any edit makes the rover Custom
- Analyze uses the numbers given, and leaving them out still gives VIPER
- Invalid numbers are refused with a message before a request is sent

## Solution
**Chosen:** `AnalyzeRequest` in `backend/app/main.py` has an optional `rover` (`solarW` > 0, `batteryWh` > 0, `loadW` ≥ 0). `analysis.analyze` scales the panel area so full Sun gives `solarW`, and passes `batteryWh` and `loadW` to `utils.simulate_battery`; the response carries the numbers used as `rover`. The front end keeps presets in `frontend/src/rovers.ts`; the classic form adds a Rover select and three fields (`components/SiteForm.tsx`), the board adds rover chips and three fields to the mission window (`board/Panels.tsx`), and the board's cache key includes the rover (`board/data.ts`). The rover readout now uses the battery size from the response rather than the highest level reached.

**Strengths**
- Three numbers anyone can find or estimate, and no new physics: the existing power and battery model is reused.
- Requests without `rover` behave exactly as before, so nothing else that calls `/analyze` changes.
- Adding a preset is one entry in `rovers.ts`.

**Limitations**
- Only one preset, VIPER, because it is the only rover found with all three numbers published or derivable. Checked and left out:

  | Rover | Published | Missing |
  |---|---|---|
  | Pragyan (Chandrayaan-3) | 50 W solar (ISRO); a 10 Ah battery (news report) | Battery voltage, so no Wh; power use |
  | Yutu-2 (Chang'e 4) | About 135 W solar in operation (secondary source) | Battery, power use |
  | Lunokhod 2 | Solar panel and batteries described, no reliable figures | All three |
  | Rashid (UAE) | Solar powered, 10 kg | All three |
  | CubeRover (Astrobotic) | "125-watt charging system" | Battery, power use |

- Power use is a single constant draw; driving and instruments are not modelled separately.
- Panel orientation and efficiency stay the defaults (6.2, 6.3).

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Presets with estimated batteries for Pragyan, Yutu-2, Lunokhod | The numbers would be guesses shown as if they were specs | More choices |
| Panel area and efficiency fields | Harder to know than a rover's solar power | Closer to the model's internals |
| Only solar power and battery, power use fixed | A fixed 108 W draw would make any small rover fail at once | One field fewer |
