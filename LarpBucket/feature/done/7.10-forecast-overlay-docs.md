## Description
Add a "Forecast overlay" section to `doc/2d-view.md` covering:
- **Field definitions:** power is Sun disc fraction × (1 − behindEarth). Comms is `clamp(margin / 0.5°, 0, 1)`.
- **The two modes:** Δ and worst dip.
- **What k steps means in hours:** at the board UI's 2 h steps, k = 6 is 12 h. At the classic UI's 1 h steps, it is 6 h.
- **Limitations:**
  - Only terrain inside the 24 km grid casts shadows or blocks Earth. The site's own `/analyze` uses a 150 km skyline, so far ridges that cover the whole area at once don't show as per-cell change.
  - The 100 m grid reads 0.5-0.8° too clear on ridges and peaks, because it misses small rises close to the rover (measured in 7.6). The pattern of change is still valid; absolute values near the marginal band are optimistic there. Proposed 7.11 is the 50 m grid.
  - This is an aid for choosing a route, not a path planner. 7.1 is the route tracer.

Mention the far-horizon limit in a short tooltip on the overlay control.

Note the overlap with 7.2 (key-factor heatmap). 7.2 shows time totals (% sunlit, % Earth visible), while this shows forward change. 7.2 can reuse `paintDelta` and the overlay controls with a sequential scale.

## Expected user interaction
The user hovers the overlay control and reads in one line what the colours mean and what the overlay can't see.

## Solution
**Chosen:** A "Forecast overlay" section in `doc/2d-view.md`, placed before the map's existing "Limits", with five parts: Controls, What is computed, How it is drawn, Speed, and Limits of the overlay. Six rows for the overlay's files were added to "Where things are", and the existing "ground is treated as 0 m high" limit now says the forecast uses the 2 m mast.

Beyond the description, the section covers what 7.9 added: the early-warning formula and its change of meaning, the 1 / 3 / 7 day spans and their sampling, the soon-to-late shades with their hex values, the hidden shadows, edge cells, and that everything runs in the browser with no endpoint.

The other two Done-when items were already met: the tooltip is on the Power and Comms buttons (7.9), and the Solution sections of 7.6 to 7.9 are filled in. The overlap with 7.2 is recorded as a line in the 7.2 ticket, where the person picking it up will see it.

**Strengths**
- Every number in the section comes from a measurement recorded in tickets 7.6 to 7.9.
- The limits are in the doc a planner would read, not only in tickets.

**Limitations**
- The figures are for the preset sites and the dates tested; other sites will differ.
- `doc/scope.md` still does not mention the overlay. The section says so.

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| A separate `doc/forecast-overlay.md` | The overlay is part of the 2D map and shares its grid, shadow sweep and limits | Shorter 2d-view.md, but two docs to keep in step |
| Add it to `doc/scope.md` | CLAUDE.md says not to change scope without the user | Would make the feature official |
