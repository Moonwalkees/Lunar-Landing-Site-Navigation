# 4.25 Rover readout on the board's detail view

Status: done
Owner: Aiken
Source: follows 4.23 (rover status card, classic UI) and 4.12 (dot-matrix board UI). Style: doc/style.md > Type, Colour, Status words.
Depends on: 4.23
Note: done. `frontend/src/board/RoverPanel.tsx`, shown over the 2D MAP, ORBIT and SURFACE views in `Detail3D.tsx`; shared logic moved to `frontend/src/rover.ts`.

## Description
The rover status card existed only in the classic UI. Show the same readout on the board's detail view, drawn in the board's style: a status word in Doto, mono captions, the battery as a row of dots, board colours.

## Expected user interaction
Open SURFACE VIEW for a site on the board. A readout sits in the top-right corner of the view: the rover's name (`VIPER (NASA) AT SITE`, or `CUSTOM ROVER AT SITE`; hover for its three numbers), a status word (`IN SUN, FULL`, `CHARGING`, `ON BATTERY`, `BATTERY EMPTY`), Sun, power and Earth (`AOS`, `MARGINAL`, `LOS`), a twelve-dot battery row, and what happens next. It changes in hard steps as time is scrubbed or played.

## Done when
- The readout shows on all three detail tabs once the site's analysis has loaded
- Its numbers equal the classic card's at the same step
- It uses only board tokens and fonts

## Solution
**Chosen:** `rover.ts` now holds the logic (`roverAt`, `roverCapacity`, `sunShare`), used by both the classic `RoverStatus.tsx` and the new `board/RoverPanel.tsx`. `BoardApp.tsx` passes the selected site's analysis and step to `Detail3D.tsx`, which draws the panel over the view. Styles are in `board.css` under `.rover-panel`.

**Strengths**
- One calculation for both UIs, so they cannot drift apart.
- Follows doc/style.md: Doto no smaller than 18 px for values, Geist Mono captions, `--ok` / `--sun` / `--selected` for the status word, unlit dots left faintly visible, no eased transitions on readouts.

**Limitations**
- `--selected` (the site-name colour) doubles as the alarm colour for `BATTERY EMPTY`; the palette has no separate one.
- Over the 2D map it sits lower, to leave the height readout its corner.
- The status words are not the board's `LIT·AOS` vocabulary. They describe the battery, which that vocabulary does not cover.

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Reuse the classic card as it is | Wrong fonts, colours and rounded corners for the board | No new file |
| Add battery columns to the board table | A bigger change to the board's layout than a readout on one view | Battery visible for every site at once |
