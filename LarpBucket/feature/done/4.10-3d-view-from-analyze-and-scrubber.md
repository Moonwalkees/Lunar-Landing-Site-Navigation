# 4.10 Drive the 3D terrain view from the analysis and the scrubber

Status: done
Owner: Aiken
Source: scope.md > Core 5 Time series (linked by a time scrubber); follows 4.9
Depends on: 4.2, 4.9

## Description
`TerrainView` takes `sun` and `earth` props (`{ az, el }` in degrees, azimuth clockwise from local north, elevation above the local level plane) and is currently given `null` for both in `App.tsx`. Once `POST /analyze` returns the Sun and Earth tracks, pass the values at the scrubber's time index, so the 3D view moves with the panorama and the time series.

Check one thing in the data: from the south pole the Earth sits toward 0° longitude, so its azimuth should be close to `360 - lon` (about 137° at Connecting Ridge). If the 3D view shows the Earth somewhere else, the azimuth conventions differ.

## Expected user interaction
After Analyze, dragging the scrubber moves the Sun and Earth in the 3D view and sweeps the shadows across the terrain, in step with the panorama markers.

## Done when
- One scrubber drives the panorama, the time series and the 3D view
- The "example angles" hint under the 3D view disappears once an analysis has run

Note: done. `App.tsx` passes the Sun and Earth angles at the scrubber index to `TerrainView`, only while the form still shows the site that was analysed.

## Solution
**Chosen:** `App.tsx` passes the Sun and Earth azimuth and elevation at the current scrubber index to `TerrainView`, which forwards them to Unity. They are passed only while the form still shows the site that was analysed.

**Strengths**
- One time index drives the 3D view, the panorama and the time series.
- The 3D view never shows one site's terrain with another site's Sun.

**Limitations**
- After the site is changed the 3D view shows example angles until Analyze is pressed again.

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Compute the Sun and Earth inside Unity | Would duplicate the backend ephemeris | The 3D view would work without the backend |
