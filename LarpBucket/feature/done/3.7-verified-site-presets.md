# 3.7 Site presets checked against published sources

Status: done
Owner: Aiken
Source: scope.md > Core 1 Observer placement (Artemis III regions, Apollo, CLPS targets); follows 3.5 and BUG-003
Note: done. `backend/app/sites.py`: every preset has a source, labels follow NASA's current list, "Peak near Shackleton" added, the Malapert point moved off a hollow; 3D terrain for the two changed areas from NASA's 5 m data.

## Description
The presets were placeholders: "(Artemis III)" on regions NASA no longer lists, a Shackleton point on a dark crater floor (BUG-003), and a Malapert point that turned out to sit in a small hollow. Check every point against a published source, label each one accurately, and add a south pole site that shows what a well-lit region looks like.

## Expected user interaction
The site list is Malapert Massif (Artemis III), Peak near Shackleton (Artemis III, 2022), Connecting Ridge (Artemis III, 2022), Shackleton crater floor, Apollo 11 and Apollo 17. Both UIs open on Malapert Massif: it is the one still on NASA's current list, ranks first on the board, and has Earth in view all year. It is also the 3D view's built-in terrain. The four south pole sites have 3D terrain; the Apollo sites are 2D only (4.28).

## Done when
- Each preset's coordinates trace to a named source
- "(Artemis III)" appears only on regions in NASA's October 2024 list; regions only on the 2022 list say so
- Every south pole preset has 3D terrain

## Solution
**Chosen:** The points, their sources, and a year of 2027 in this app's model (2 m mast, VIPER rover defaults):

| Preset | Lat, lon | Source | 2027 here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connecting Ridge (Artemis III, 2022) | -89.45, 222.7 | Crest of NASA LOLA 5 m "Site01: Connecting ridge" (NASA PGDA, Barker et al. 2021; numbering after Mazarico et al. 2011) | 76.2% sunlit, longest night 256 h |
| Peak near Shackleton (Artemis III, 2022) | -88.8081, 123.718 | Highest point of NASA LOLA 5 m "Site07: Peak near Shackleton"; 0.7 km from the spot Mahanti et al. 2024 (PSJ 5:62) give as lit about 80% of the time, citing Mazarico et al. 2011 | 87.9% sunlit, longest night 248 h |
| Malapert Massif (Artemis III) | -85.964, 357.681 | NASA's notional Artemis III site on Mons Malapert, Kring et al. 2025 (JGR Planets) | 71.0% sunlit, Earth in view 100% |
| Shackleton crater floor | -89.67, 129.78 | Crater centre, IAU Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature (USGS) | 0% sunlit |
| Apollo 11 | 0.67408, 23.47297 | Lunar module, NASA NSSDC Apollo landing site table | 51.6% sunlit (2026-27), Earth 100% |
| Apollo 17 | 20.1908, 30.77168 | Lunar module, NASA NSSDC Apollo landing site table | 49.6% sunlit (2026-27), Earth 100% |

Region lists: NASA named 13 Artemis III candidate regions in August 2022 (including Connecting Ridge and Peak Near Shackleton) and narrowed them to 9 in October 2024 (Peak near Cabeus B, Haworth, Malapert Massif, Mons Mouton Plateau, Mons Mouton, Nobile Rim 1, Nobile Rim 2, de Gerlache Rim 2, Slater Plain).

3D terrain: Peak near Shackleton and Malapert now use NASA's LOLA 5 m Site07 and Site23 DEMs (`frontend/src/terrain.ts`, `doc/3d-view.md`). The old Malapert tile came from the 20 m map and did not reach the new point. `frontend/public/unity/` grows from 55 MB to 63 MB; only the area being viewed is downloaded.

**Strengths**
- Every point traces to NASA data or a published paper, and the labels match NASA's current list.
- The model agrees with independent figures where they exist: Peak near Shackleton 87.9% against a published "about 80%", and Malapert's Earth in view 100% against its known constant Earth visibility.
- The board now has a clearly lit site (Peak near Shackleton), a candidate on NASA's current list (Malapert), a dark crater floor and two equatorial Apollo sites for contrast.

**Limitations**
- The Peak near Shackleton point is the peak in NASA's terrain, not the published coordinate. The published (-88.79, 124.5) is rounded to about ±300 m and falls on the slope, where the model gives 51%.
- The Kring et al. 2025 coordinates were read from the paper's text as quoted by a search index; the publisher's page blocks automated downloads. The paper's title and subject were confirmed through Crossref.
- The Connecting Ridge point is the crest of NASA's Site01 terrain; no published paper was found that gives exactly this point.
- No CLPS target yet, although scope.md lists them.
- The "(Artemis III, 2022)" label is short; the meaning is in `sites.py` and this ticket.

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Use the published Peak near Shackleton coordinate as given | On the slope, 51% sunlit here | Exactly the paper's number |
| Malapert summit (highest point of Site23) | 67.6% sunlit and Earth 79.2% here, against 71.0% and 100% at NASA's notional site | The literal summit |
| Add NASA's 2024 regions (Haworth, de Gerlache Rim 2, Nobile Rim) | Each needs new terrain, a wider back-end map and a rebuild | NASA's current shortlist |
| Drop the Apollo presets | scope.md lists Apollo; they show the contrast with the pole | Every preset with 3D terrain |
