## Description
Add a panel that shows the real LOLA terrain around the selected site in 3D, at true scale, with the Sun casting shadows and the Earth in the sky. It is a pre-built Unity WebGL view embedded in the React app. It only displays what it is told (site, Sun and Earth azimuth and elevation); it never computes them.

Detailed terrain exists for two 16 km areas, Connecting Ridge and Shackleton rim, each wrapped in coarser terrain out to 160 km.

## Expected user interaction
The user picks a site. If it lies in one of the two areas, the 3D view moves there (downloading that area's terrain if needed); otherwise the panel says there is no 3D terrain for the site yet. The user drags to look around, scrolls to zoom, switches between an orbit view and a view standing on the surface, and can load finer terrain with **High detail**.

Until `POST /analyze` exists the Sun and Earth sit at example angles, and the panel says so. Wiring them to the analysis and the scrubber is ticket 4.10.

## Done when
- `npm run build` passes
- The panel renders with the other five panels and follows the site form
- Typing in the site form still works while the 3D view is running
- A site outside both areas shows a message, not a wrong location

## Solution
**Chosen:** `frontend/src/components/TerrainView.tsx` embeds a Unity WebGL build served from `frontend/public/unity/` and sends it the site and the Sun and Earth angles (`SetState`). `frontend/src/terrain.ts` lists the four terrain tiles and picks the one that contains the site. Notes for the team are in `doc/3d-view.md`.

**Strengths**
- Real cast shadows under a Sun 1.5 degrees up; they agree with an independent horizon calculation over 95% of the terrain.
- The Sun can move every frame without stutter, which is what the time scrubber needs.

**Limitations**
- Not in scope.md or the agreed stack. It needs the team's agreement, and doc/style.md lists a dot-matrix 3D map as an open decision.
- Adds 68 MB to `frontend/public/unity/`.
- Terrain only for Connecting Ridge, Shackleton rim, Shackleton crater and Malapert Massif. Other sites show a note in place of terrain.
- The build comes from a separate Unity project that is not in this repo.
- Not tested on a weak laptop or a real phone; no pinch zoom.

**Alternatives considered**
| Option | Why not chosen | Trade-off vs chosen |
|---|---|---|
| three.js | Built and compared: shadows updated more slowly when the Sun moves | No 68 MB build, and easier to restyle as dot-matrix |
| No 3D view, panorama only | The owner wanted to see where the shadows fall on the ground | Stays inside the agreed scope |
